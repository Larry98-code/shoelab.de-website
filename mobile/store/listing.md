## App Store "App Privacy" answers

Data **linked to the user**, used for **App Functionality** only, **no tracking**:

| Type | Collected because |
|---|---|
| Contact info — name, email address, phone, physical address | The account; delivery address for orders |
| User content — photos | Listings, Legit Checks, reviews |
| Identifiers — user ID, device ID (push token) | The account; notifications |
| Purchases — purchase history | Orders in the Vault |
| Other — messages to the seller (offer notes), reports | Offers and reports |

Not collected: location, health, financial info (cards are handled by Stripe on Stripe's page), browsing history, diagnostics, advertising data. **No tracking** (no ads, no analytics SDKs).

## Google Play "Data safety" answers

- Data collected: name, email, address, phone (Personal info); photos (Photos and videos); purchase history (Financial info → purchase history); app interactions — no; device or other IDs (push token).
- All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Members can delete their account in the app (Settings → Delete my account).
- No data shared with third parties for their own purposes; processors: Cloudflare (hosting), Stripe (payments), Resend (email), Expo (push delivery), Anthropic (the Lab Check, photos only).
