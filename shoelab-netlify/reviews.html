How reviews work here

  • “Verified” means the review is linked to a real booking, Vault order or Legit Check of the person who wrote it. Reviews without that link are shown as not verified.
  • We publish every genuine review, positive or negative, after a short check — usually within 24 hours. We only turn a review down for insults, other people’s personal data, spam, a duplicate, or if it is not about ShoeLab.
  • Nobody is paid, rewarded or offered a discount for a review, and we never edit what customers write. We may reply below a review.
  • The score above is the average of all reviews published on this page. Reviews on Google or Instagram are not included.
