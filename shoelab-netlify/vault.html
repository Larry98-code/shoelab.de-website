Good to know

Questions, answered

How does automatic bidding work?
You enter the most you would pay. The Vault bids for you, one step at a time, only as far as it takes to keep you in the lead. Nobody sees your maximum.
What stops last-second sniping?
A bid in the final two minutes extends the auction by two minutes, so everyone always gets a fair chance to answer.
What if a pair fails authentication?
A Lab Verified pair that fails our inspection never ships to you, and your payment is refunded in full. The pair goes back to the seller.
Something is wrong with my delivery. What now?
Report it from My Vault before you confirm delivery. The seller's payout is put on hold and a ShoeLab specialist looks into it.
When does the seller get paid?
Once the buyer confirms delivery, or automatically fourteen days after shipping if no problem is reported.
Are bids binding?
Yes. Winning an auction, accepting an offer or pressing Buy Now is a commitment to buy. Pay within 72 hours to secure the pair.

Got a pair sitting in its box?

List it in the Vault in under five minutes. Listing is free — you only pay when it sells.
