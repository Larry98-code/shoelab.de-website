FAQ

Good to know

Is the answer 100% certain?
No check from photos can promise that — anyone who does is guessing. Ours is strict: it compares every detail, asks for what it cannot see, and a specialist decides. If we cannot reach a sure answer, you get your money back.
I don't have the pair yet. Does it still work?
Yes. Upload the seller's photos or screenshots of the listing. If something important is missing, we tell you what to ask the seller for — with a ready-made message you can send them.
How do buyers check my certificate?
Add the certificate picture as the last photo of your listing. Buyers type its number at shoelabonline.de/verified and see the photos we checked. If the pair in your listing is different, they will notice.
Which apps does it work with?
All of them: Vinted, Kleinanzeigen, eBay, Depop, Grailed, StockX, Facebook, Instagram and any other. Paste the link, or on Android share the listing straight to ShoeLab once you have added this page to your home screen.
Who sees my photos?
Only you and our specialists. If you publish a certificate, its photos are public — photos you send on request, like a receipt, never are.
